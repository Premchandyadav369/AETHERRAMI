The "Explain Like I'm 5" (ELI5) Guide

1. The Lock and Key Problem

Diseases happen when bad proteins in your body act like jammed locks. Medicines are like specially crafted keys made to turn off those jammed locks.

2. Why Normal Medicines Stop Working (Drug Resistance)

Bacteria and cancer cells mutate. They change the shape of the keyhole just a tiny bit. Old keys (like Isoniazid or Sotorasib) don't fit anymore, so the patient relapses.

3. The 16B MoE Supercomputer Solution

AETHER-RAMI has 8 specialized AI experts inside a 16.4 Billion parameter brain. A router picks the 2 best experts for the job, designing a master key that works on both original and mutated locks, while keeping healthy cells completely unharmed.
