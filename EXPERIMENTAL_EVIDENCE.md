# AETHER-RAMI: Experimental Evidence & Preclinical Discovery Consortium Validation Status
## Grounded Evidence Taxonomy, Wet-Lab Validation Protocol, and Portfolio Manifest
**Audited Target Portfolio:** 24 Disease Targets | **Candidate Library:** 101 Prioritized Leads

---

### 1. Scientific Evidence Classification Framework

To maintain absolute scientific integrity, all data within AETHER-RAMI is partitioned into four explicit evidence states:

```
[Level 1: Computationally Predicted] 
   └── Multi-objective in silico scoring, SE(3) diffusion, Langevin MD, ADMET QSAR models.
[Level 2: Preclinical Submission Ready] 
   └── Fully packaged in 96/384-well plate layouts, SOPs, and CoA for prospective lab testing (Preclinical Discovery Consortium).
[Level 3: Reference Control Benchmarked] 
   └── Historical reference compounds benchmarked against literature in vitro IC50 / Kd values.
[Level 4: Clinically Validated] 
   └── FDA-approved benchmark drugs used as positive controls (e.g., Osimertinib, Sitagliptin).
```

### 2. Preclinical Discovery Consortium Hyderabad Experimental Submission Package

A complete, standards-compliant wet-lab validation submission package was prepared and structured for the **National Preclinical Discovery Consortium (Wet-Lab)**, Hyderabad:

1. **Master Sample Manifest (`01_National Laboratory_Wet-Lab_Master_Sample_Manifest_and_Testing_Worklist.xlsx`):**
   * Complete metadata for all 101 candidate molecules across the 24 disease targets.
   * Chemical structures (SMILES, InChIKey), Molecular Weights, purity targets ($> 95\%$), recommended DMSO solvent stock concentrations (10 mM).
2. **Assay Data Entry & IC50 Calculations (`02_National Laboratory_Wet-Lab_Assay_Data_Entry_and_Calculations.xlsx`):**
   * Standard 8-point 3-fold serial dilution assay plates (10 µM down to 4.5 nM).
   * Automated non-linear 4-parameter logistic (4PL) Hill equation fitting template for exact $IC_{50}$ determination.
3. **Synthesis Batch Log & Certificate of Analysis (`03_National Laboratory_Wet-Lab_Synthesis_Batch_Log_and_CoA.xlsx`):**
   * Synthetic batch tracking, HPLC-MS retention times, $^1	ext{H}$-NMR chemical shift characterization, and storage requirements ($-20^\circ	ext{C}$ desiccated).
4. **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):**
   * `SOP_SYN_01`: Automated Enoyl-ACP Reductase (InhA) Fluorometric NADH Assay.
   * `SOP_SYN_02`: EGFR T790M / WT Kinase Selectivity TR-FRET Assay.
   * `SOP_SYN_03`: KRAS G12C Covalent Mass Spectrometry Intact Protein Adduct Assay.
   * `SOP_SYN_04`: Automated Liquid Handler 384-Well Plate Dispensing Protocol.
