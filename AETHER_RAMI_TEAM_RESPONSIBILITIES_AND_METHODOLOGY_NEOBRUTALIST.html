1. SE(3)-Equivariant Continuous Flow Matching ODE (Premchand Yadav)
dx_t / dt = v_θ(x_t, t, c) where v_θ(R·x_t + t, t, R·c + t) = R · v_θ(x_t, t, c)
2. Sparse MoE Top-2 Gating Router with Gaussian Exploration (Premchand Yadav)
G(h)_i = Softmax(Top2(W_g · h + ε)), ε ~ N(0, σ^2) ──> y = Σ G(h)_i · E_i(h)
3. Analytical Steric Clash Repulsion Force Field (Sri Harsha Vardhan)
-∇ E_steric = +2 · δ_ij · ((x_i - x_j) / ||x_i - x_j||) where δ_ij = (r_i^vdW + r_j^vdW) - ||x_i - x_j|| > 0
4. Resistance Mutation Invariance Loss (Premchand Yadav & Sai Praneeth)
L_bypass = |ΔG_pred(x, P_mut) - ΔG_pred(x, P_WT)| + λ · max(0, ΔG_pred(x, P_mut) - ΔG_thresh)
