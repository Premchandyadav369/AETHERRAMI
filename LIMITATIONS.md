# AETHER-RAMI: Scientific, Computational & Experimental Limitations
## Transparent Disclosure of System Boundaries, Modeling Assumptions, and Failure Modes
**Document Release:** v4.2.0-Landed | **Status:** FORMAL_DISCLOSURE

---

### 1. Biophysical & Structural Modeling Assumptions
* **Implicit vs. Explicit Solvation:** While the landed system executes fast Langevin dynamics and dielectric screening ($\epsilon = 4.0$ protein, $\epsilon = 78.4$ solvent), it does not simulate explicit TIP3P water boxes across microsecond timescales. Critical water-mediated bridging hydrogen bonds must be verified via crystallographic inspection.
* **Protein Backbone Plasticity:** The pocket-conditioned generation evaluates flexible ligand conformers against semi-rigid receptor backbone scaffolds. Large domain hinge bending or induced-fit structural rearrangements are not modeled during the initial diffusion step.
* **Covalent Chemistry Modeling:** Covalent warhead docking (e.g., acrylamides on Cys12 in KRAS or Cys797 in EGFR) uses proximity and geometry constraints rather than quantum-mechanical transition-state barrier evaluations.

### 2. Systems Pharmacology & Transcriptomics Limitations
* **Cellular Line Linearity:** The cellular perturbation network (`RAMI-CellPerturb-scGPT`) models transcriptomic log2 fold changes based on 32 sentinel hallmark genes mapped to LINCS L1000 patterns. It predicts gene modulation trends rather than absolute RNA copy numbers.
* **Off-Target Proteome Scale:** Selectivity scoring evaluates designated antitargets (e.g., hERG, WT-EGFR, homologous kinome panels). It does not test the entire 20,000-protein human proteome.

### 3. Retrosynthetic Route Execution Limitations
* **Predicted vs. Physical Synthesis:** Retrosynthetic plans generated by `AETHER-SynthonSpace-MoE` are algorithmic disconnections using validated reaction SMARTS templates. A predicted 75% yield does not guarantee physical lab success without empirical reaction optimization.
* **Solvent & Catalyst Specifics:** Specific reagent combinations (e.g., HATU, DIPEA, $	ext{Pd(dppf)Cl}_2$) represent standard literature conditions; actual wet-lab synthesis may require catalyst screening for sterically hindered substrates.

### 4. Computational Hardware Profile
* **CPU vs. GPU Optimization:** The landed v4.2.0 package is optimized for CPU execution. While highly efficient (~1 s / target), distributed large-scale library screening ($> 100,000$ molecules) requires deploying on GPU clusters using the 16.8B theoretical scaling blueprint.
