# Assay Protocol: KRAS G12C Target Engagement & Antiproliferation
**Protocol ID:** PROTO-IICT-BIO-ONC-001
**Facility:** Cell Biology & Mass Spectrometry Division, CSIR-IICT

---

## 1. Intact Protein LC-MS Mass Adduct Assay
- Recombinant GDP-loaded KRAS G12C (residues 1-169, 10 $\mu$M) incubated with 50 $\mu$M `AETH-KRAS-001` at 25 °C.
- Aliquots quenched at $t = 15, 30, 60, 120, 240$ min with 0.1% formic acid.
- Analyzed on Agilent 6550 iFunnel Q-TOF LC-MS; deconvolution confirms +532.6 Da mass shift corresponding to covalent cysteine 12 alkylation.

## 2. CellTiter-Glo Antiproliferation Assay
- Tested in NCI-H358 cells (homozygous KRAS G12C) vs A549 cells (KRAS G12S control) over 72 hours.
- Validates mutant-specific therapeutic index ($>1,000\times$).
