# Assay Protocol: Mtb H37Rv Microplate Alamar Blue Assay (MABA)
**Protocol ID:** PROTO-IICT-BIO-TB-001
**Facility:** BSL-3 High-Containment Tuberculosis Laboratory, CSIR-IICT

---

## 1. Assay Principle & Methodology
- Uses resazurin dye to assess viability of *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* strain H37Rv (ATCC 27294) and clinical MDR isolates.
- Non-fluorescent blue resazurin is reduced by viable metabolically active mycobacteria to pink, highly fluorescent resorufin (Ex 530 nm / Em 590 nm).
- Serial dilutions of `AETH-INHA-002` evaluated from 0.01 $\mu$g/mL to 64 $\mu$g/mL.
- MIC90 defined as lowest compound concentration preventing color shift from blue to pink (or $>90\%$ inhibition of resorufin fluorescence).
