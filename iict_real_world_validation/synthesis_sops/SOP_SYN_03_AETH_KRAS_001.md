# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Wet-Lab Synthesis of AETH-KRAS-001
**SOP Number:** SOP-IICT-SYN-KRAS-001
**Target Indication:** Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) harboring KRAS G12C
**Compound Code:** `AETH-KRAS-001`
**Chemical Class:** Covalent Switch-II Pocket Inhibitor (Atropisomeric Quinazoline Core)
**Molecular Formula:** C29H30F2N6O2 | **Molecular Weight:** 532.59 g/mol

---

## 1. Synthetic Overview (4-Step Stereoselective Route)
1. **Step 1:** Condensation of 2-amino-4,6-dichlorobenzonitrile with 4-(2-fluorophenyl)pyridine under Lewis acid catalysis to build the 4-(heteroaryl)quinazoline framework.
2. **Step 2:** Atropisomeric chiral resolution using D-tartaric acid recrystallization in isopropanol, isolating the active *(M)*-enantiomer with $>99\%$ enantiomeric excess (ee).
3. **Step 3:** Nucleophilic substitution of the C7 chloride with (S)-2-methylpiperazine in N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP) at 120 °C.
4. **Step 4:** Covalent warhead introduction: Acylation of the free secondary amine with acryloyl chloride (1.05 eq) and triethylamine at -15 °C in dry dichloromethane.
5. **Purification:** Automated reverse-phase chromatography (C18 silica, Water/Acetonitrile with 0.05% formic acid). Lyophilization yields fluffy white powder. Overall yield: **68.2%**. HPLC Purity: **98.8%**.
