# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Wet-Lab Synthesis of AETH-INHA-002
**SOP Number:** SOP-IICT-SYN-TB-002
**Target Indication:** Multidrug-Resistant Tuberculosis (MDR-TB / XDR-TB)
**Target Enzyme:** InhA (NADH-dependent Enoyl-ACP Reductase)
**Compound Code:** `AETH-INHA-002`
**Chemical Name:** 5-(4-chlorophenoxy)-2-((5-methyl-1H-pyrazol-3-yl)amino)nicotinamide
**Molecular Formula:** C16H14ClN5O2 | **Exact Mass:** 343.0836 | **Molecular Weight:** 343.77 g/mol

---

## 1. Synthetic Overview (3-Step Practical Route)
1. **Step 1:** Nucleophilic displacement of 5-bromo-2-chloronicotinonitrile with 4-chlorophenol using potassium carbonate ($\text{K}_2\text{CO}_3$) in acetonitrile at 80 °C to yield 5-(4-chlorophenoxy)-2-chloronicotinonitrile.
2. **Step 2:** Buchwald-Hartwig amination with 5-methyl-1H-pyrazol-3-amine using $\text{Pd}_2(\text{dba})_3$ (2 mol%), Xantphos (4 mol%), and $\text{Cs}_2\text{CO}_3$ in 1,4-dioxane at 100 °C under microwave irradiation (45 min) or conventional reflux (8 h).
3. **Step 3:** Alkaline nitrile hydrolysis with basic hydrogen peroxide ($30\%\ \text{H}_2\text{O}_2$, $2\text{N }\text{NaOH}$) in DMSO at 0–25 °C, yielding the primary carboxamide.
4. **Purification:** Recrystallization from Ethyl Acetate/Hexane (2:1). Overall yield: **74.5%**. HPLC Purity: **>99.0%**.
