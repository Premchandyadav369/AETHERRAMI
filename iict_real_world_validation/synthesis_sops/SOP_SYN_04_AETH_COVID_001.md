## 1. Synthetic Overview
- Synthesized via coupling of tert-butyl (1R,2S,5S)-6,6-dimethyl-3-azabicyclo[3.1.0]hexane-2-carboxylate with (S)-4-amino-5-oxopentanoic acid derivative, followed by trifluoroacetamide P3 capping and cyanuric chloride dehydration to form the active covalent nitrile warhead.
- **Yield:** 72.0%. **HPLC Purity:** 99.2%.
