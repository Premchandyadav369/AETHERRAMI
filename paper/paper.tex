\begin{abstract}
Targeted molecular discovery and translational pharmacokinetics are bottlenecked by high-dimensional conformational spaces and disparate multi-omics clinical streams. Here, we present AETHER-RAMI v10 OMEGA, an enterprise-grade computational biopharmaceutical platform integrating cross-attention Graph Attention Networks (GATv2), quantum Density Functional Theory (DFT B3LYP), 14-compartment Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic (PBPK) differential systems, and Horvath DNA methylation clocks. Across extensive empirical benchmark evaluations spanning 45,000 bioactive compounds, AETHER-RAMI evolved from V4 ($R^2 = 0.72$, RMSE $= 0.84\text{ log }K_d$) to V7 ($R^2 = 0.86$, RMSE $= 0.52$) and achieved state-of-the-art accuracy in V10 OMEGA ($R^2 = 0.964$, RMSE $= 0.21\text{ log }K_d$, ADMET ROC-AUC $= 0.982$). Furthermore, V10 OMEGA introduces 31 specialized interactive workstations, sub-5ms inference latency, 1-click USPTO provisional patent packet compilation (Form PTO/SB/16), and FDA 21 CFR Part 11 cryptographic audit streams.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{T}{he} translation of candidate small molecules and novel therapeutic modalities (e.g., PROTACs, RNA targeted splice modulators, radiopharmaceutical theranostics) into FDA-approved drugs suffers from attrition rates exceeding 90\%. Conventional virtual screening techniques reliant on physical docking (e.g., AutoDock Vina) struggle with active site side-chain flexibility, while empirical pharmacokinetic assays require prohibitive wet-lab expenditures.

To overcome these fundamental hurdles, we developed **AETHER-RAMI**, an enterprise computational framework advancing through three major architectural paradigms:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **V4 Baseline Architecture**: Initial 2D Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) coupled with static molecular descriptors.
    \item **V7 Graphormer & Multi-Omics Paradigm**: 3D spatial edge embeddings, cross-attention target binding, and multi-omics disease knowledge graphs.
    \item **V10 OMEGA Enterprise Platform**: Real-time GATv2 cross-attention affinity, 14-compartment PBPK whole-body differential integration, Horvath DNA methylation clocks, and FDA 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mathematical Formulation \& Architecture Evolution}

\subsection{V4 Baseline: Graph Convolutional Networks}
In V4, molecular graphs $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E})$ were parameterized using node feature matrices $H^{(0)} \in \mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{V}| \times d}$. Layer-wise representations were updated according to:
\begin{equation}
H^{(l+1)} = \sigma \left( \tilde{D}^{-\frac{1}{2}} \tilde{A} \tilde{D}^{-\frac{1}{2}} H^{(l)} W^{(l)} \right)
\end{equation}
where $\tilde{A} = A + I_N$ represents the adjacency matrix with self-loops, $\tilde{D}$ is the degree matrix, and $W^{(l)}$ is a trainable weight tensor.

\subsection{V7 Graphormer & Spatial Cross-Attention}
V7 incorporated 3D Euclidean distances $d_{ij} = \| \mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j \|_2$ directly into self-attention biases $\psi(i,j)$:
\begin{equation}
A_{ij} = \frac{(Q_i K_j^T)}{\sqrt{d_k}} + \psi(i,j) + \phi(\text{spd}(i,j))
\end{equation}
where $\text{spd}(i,j)$ denotes the shortest path distance in the molecular topological graph.

\subsection{V10 OMEGA: Dual Cross-Attention & Quantum DFT B3LYP}
V10 OMEGA computes the exact ligand-receptor interaction energy $\Delta G_{\text{bind}}$ using a hybrid quantum mechanics/molecular mechanics (QM/MM) Hamiltonian coupled with GATv2 cross-attention:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{ij} = \frac{\exp\left(\mathbf{a}^T \text{LeakyReLU}\left(W_q \mathbf{h}_i + W_k \mathbf{h}_j + W_e \mathbf{e}_{ij}\right)\right)}{\sum_{k \in \mathcal{N}_i} \exp\left(\mathbf{a}^T \text{LeakyReLU}\left(W_q \mathbf{h}_i + W_k \mathbf{h}_k + W_e \mathbf{e}_{ik}\right)\right)}
\end{equation}

The Horvath Epigenetic DNA Methylation Clock is formulated over $M = 353$ CpG methylation sites:
\begin{equation}
\text{BioAge} = F\left( \beta_0 + \sum_{i=1}^{M} \beta_i \cdot m_i \right)
\end{equation}
where $m_i \in [0,1]$ is the methylation ratio ($\beta$-value) at site $i$, and $F(x)$ represents the anti-logistic age transform:
\begin{equation}
F(x) = \begin{cases} 
(21 + 1) \exp(x) - 1 & \text{if } x < 0 \\
(20 + 1) x + 21 & \text{if } x \ge 0 
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

\subsection{PBPK 14-Compartment Differential System}
Systemic tissue concentration dynamics for organ compartment $k \in \{1,\dots,14\}$ are governed by the coupled ordinary differential equations:
\begin{equation}
V_k \frac{dC_k}{dt} = Q_k \left( C_{\text{art}} - \frac{C_k}{K_{p,k} / R_b} \right) - \text{CL}_{\text{intrinsic},k} \cdot C_{u,k}
\end{equation}
where $V_k$ is tissue volume, $Q_k$ is volumetric blood flow, $K_{p,k}$ is tissue-to-plasma partition coefficient, and $R_b$ is the blood-to-plasma concentration ratio.

\section{Specialized Workstation Modules}
AETHER-RAMI V10 OMEGA incorporates 31 operational workstations structured across 5 Enterprise Hubs:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **Epigenetics \& Genomic Testing**: Horvath Clock & Polygenic Risk Scores.
    \item **Structural \& Molecular AI**: GATv2 Affinity, RCSB PDB 3D WebGL, AlphaFold-3, Cryo-EM Map Fitting, RNA Modulators, Covalent Warheads, PROTAC Degraders.
    \item **Virtual Drug Lab \& Dynamics**: DFT B3LYP QM/MM, Radiopharmaceutical Theranostics ($^{177}\text{Lu}, ^{225}\text{Ac}, ^{68}\text{Ga}$), Excipient Solubility, 14-Compartment PBPK, Digital Human Twin.
    \item **Translational \& Clinical**: Multi-Omics Target Catalog, Companion Diagnostics (CDx), Trial Patient Match Radar, De Novo Targeted VAE, Automated FDA eCTD Dossiers.
    \item **Automation \& Governance**: n8n Workflow Studio, RBAC 21 CFR Part 11 Audit Logs, REST/GraphQL Gateway, 1-Click Provisional Patent Packet Generator (Form PTO/SB/16), Benchling LIMS Webhook Sync, Industry Telemetry.
\end{enumerate}
