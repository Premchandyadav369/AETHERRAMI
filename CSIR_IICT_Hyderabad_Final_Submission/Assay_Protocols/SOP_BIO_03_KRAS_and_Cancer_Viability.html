Target System: KRAS G12C Switch-II Pocket Covalent Target Conducting Facility: Centre for Chemical Biology, Bio-Organic Mass Spectrometry & Molecular Oncology Lab
Sample Allocation: VAL-CMPD-03, VAL-CMPD-15, and Reference Control REF-CTRL-07 (Sotorasib Reference)

1. Purpose & Scope

To measure the rate and extent of irreversible covalent bond formation between electrophilic acrylamide test inhibitors and Cysteine-12 of recombinant GDP-bound KRAS G12C, followed by cellular antiproliferative validation in KRAS G12C mutant non-small cell lung cancer cells (NCI-H358).

2. Part A: Intact Protein ESI-TOF Mass Spectrometry Covalent Kinetics

  1. Protein Preparation: Recombinant human KRAS G12C (amino acids 1-169, GDP-loaded) at 5.0 μM in 20 mM HEPES (pH 7.5), 150 mM NaCl, 1.0 mM MgCl2.
  2. Reaction Incubation: Incubate KRAS G12C protein with 10.0 μM test compound (VAL-CMPD-03, REF-CTRL-07) at 25 °C. Quench aliquots at t = 15, 30, 60, and 120 minutes with 0.5% formic acid.
  3. LC-MS Desalting & Acquisition: Inject 5 μL on an Agilent 6545 Q-TOF LC/MS equipped with a PLRP-S column (1000 Å, 5 μm, 2.1 x 50 mm). Run rapid desalting gradient (5% to 90% MeCN + 0.1% FA over 3 minutes at 0.5 mL/min).
  4. Deconvolution: Deconvolute intact mass spectra using Agilent MassHunter Maximum Entropy algorithm (mass range: 18,000 to 22,000 Da).
  5. Quantification: Compute % covalent modification:
    % Adduct = [Area(KRAS + Adduct) / (Area(Unmodified KRAS) + Area(KRAS + Adduct))] x 100.
    Target cutoff: > 95% covalent adduct formation at 60 minutes.

3. Part B: NCI-H358 Cellular Antiproliferation Assay (CellTiter-Glo)

  1. Cell Lines: NCI-H358 (heterozygous KRAS G12C mutant lung adenocarcinoma) and A549 (KRAS G12S control lung carcinoma for selectivity assessment).
  2. Seeding: Seed 2,000 cells/well in 90 μL complete RPMI-1640 (10% FBS) in white 96-well optical plates. Incubate overnight at 37 °C, 5% CO2.
  3. Treatment: Add 10 μL of 10x compound serial dilutions (0.1 nM to 10,000 nM; final 0.1% DMSO). Incubate for 72 hours.
  4. Viability Readout: Equilibrate plates to 25 °C for 30 minutes. Add 100 μL CellTiter-Glo reagent. Shake for 2 minutes and incubate for 10 minutes. Read luminescence on a microplate luminometer. Compute GI50 using non-linear curve fitting.
