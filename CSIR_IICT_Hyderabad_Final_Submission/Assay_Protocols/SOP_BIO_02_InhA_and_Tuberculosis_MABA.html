STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: IN-VITRO PHARMACOLOGY
SOP Number: SOP-BIO-02 Effective Date: October 31, 2026
Target System: Mycobacterium tuberculosis InhA Enoyl-ACP Reductase Conducting Facility: Centre for Chemical Biology (CCB), BSL-3 Mycobacterial Testing Unit
Sample Allocation: VAL-CMPD-02, VAL-CMPD-14, and Reference Control REF-CTRL-06 (Isoniazid IP)

1. Purpose & Scope

To evaluate direct binding inhibitors of Mycobacterium tuberculosis InhA that act independently of catalase-peroxidase (KatG) activation, circumventing primary clinical isoniazid resistance.

2. Safety & Containment

  • Mycobacterial culture work MUST be conducted within certified Biosafety Level 3 (BSL-3) negative pressure suites with dedicated biosafety cabinets (Class II Type A2/B2).
  • N95/P100 respirators and full protective suits mandatory.

3. Part A: Recombinant InhA Spectrophotometric Kinetic Assay

  1. Principle: InhA catalyzes the NADH-dependent reduction of 2-trans-enoyl-ACP. Reaction velocity is quantified by the decrease in absorbance at 340 nm (Δε340 = 6.22 mM-1cm-1) using trans-2-dodecenoyl-CoA (DD-CoA) as substrate analog.
  2. Reaction Mixture: 30 mM PIPES (pH 6.8), 100 mM NaCl, 0.05% Tween-20, 200 μM NADH, 50 μM DD-CoA, and 50 nM recombinant InhA.
  3. Execution: Pre-incubate enzyme with test compound (VAL-CMPD-02 or VAL-CMPD-14, 0.1 nM to 10 μM) for 15 minutes at 25 °C. Add DD-CoA substrate and record A340 for 5 minutes. Calculate IC50 and direct-binding inhibition percentage.

4. Part B: M. tuberculosis H37Rv Microplate Alamar Blue Assay (MABA)

  1. Inoculum Preparation: Culture M. tuberculosis H37Rv in Middlebrook 7H9 broth supplemented with 0.2% glycerol, 0.05% Tween-80, and 10% OADC enrichment. Adjust turbidity to McFarland standard 1.0 (approx. 3 x 107 CFU/mL) and dilute 1:20 in 7H9 broth.
  2. Microplate Setup: Dispense 100 μL media in sterile 96-well plates. Add 100 μL compound stock in row A and perform 2-fold serial dilutions down to row G (0.01 μg/mL to 64 μg/mL). Add 100 μL mycobacterial inoculum to all test wells. Include growth controls (no drug) and sterile media blanks.
  3. Incubation: Seal plates with breathable tape and incubate at 37 °C in 5% CO2 for 7 days.
  4. Alamar Blue Addition: On day 7, add 25 μL of freshly prepared 1:1 mixture of Alamar Blue reagent and 10% sterile Tween-80. Incubate for additional 24 hours at 37 °C.
  5. Evaluation: Visual readout: Blue = No growth (Inhibition); Pink = Bacterial growth. Quantitative fluorescence: Ex 530 nm / Em 590 nm. MIC90 is defined as the lowest drug concentration preventing color shift from blue to pink.
