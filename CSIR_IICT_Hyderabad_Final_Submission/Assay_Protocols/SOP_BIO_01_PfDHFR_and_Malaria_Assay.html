STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: IN-VITRO PHARMACOLOGY
SOP Number: SOP-BIO-01 Effective Date: October 31, 2026
Target System: Plasmodium falciparum Dihydrofolate Reductase-Thymidylate Synthase (PfDHFR-TS) Conducting Facility: Centre for Chemical Biology (CCB), BSL-2 Malaria Culture Laboratory
Sample Allocation: VAL-CMPD-01, VAL-CMPD-13, and Reference Control REF-CTRL-05 (Pyrimethamine USP)

1. Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the validated protocol for determining the in-vitro enzymatic inhibition (IC50, Ki) of test compounds against recombinant wild-type and quadruple-mutant (N51I/C59R/S108N/I164L) Plasmodium falciparum DHFR-TS, as well as whole-cell antimalarial potency against chloroquine-sensitive (3D7) and multidrug-resistant (Dd2) parasite strains.

2. Safety & Facility Requirements

  • Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) containment required for whole-cell blood stage parasite cultures.
  • Use standard PPE: Lab coat, double nitrile gloves, and eye protection. All human erythrocytes must be screened and certified negative for HIV, HBV, and HCV.

3. Part A: Recombinant Enzyme Spectrophotometric Kinetic Assay

  1. Reaction Principle: Dihydrofolate reductase converts 7,8-dihydrofolate (H2folate) and NADPH to 5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate (H4folate) and NADP+. Reaction progress is continuously monitored by measuring the decrease in optical density at 340 nm (NADPH extinction coefficient Δε340 = 6.22 mM-1cm-1).
  2. Assay Buffer: 50 mM TES (pH 7.0), 75 mM β-mercaptoethanol, 1.0 mM EDTA, 10% (v/v) glycerol, 0.1 mg/mL Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA).
  3. Substrate Cocktail: 50 μM Dihydrofolic acid (H2folate) + 100 μM NADPH in assay buffer.
  4. Plate Setup: UV-transparent 96-well microplate (Corning 3635). Dispense 180 μL of buffer containing recombinant enzyme (1.5 nM final) and 10-point serial dilutions of test compound (0.01 nM to 1,000 nM; final DMSO ≤ 0.5%). Pre-incubate at 25 °C for 10 minutes.
  5. Initiation & Readout: Initiate reaction by dispensing 20 μL substrate cocktail. Read A340 continuously for 300 seconds (kinetic interval: 10 seconds) at 25 °C on a SpectraMax i3x reader.
  6. Calculations: Compute initial velocity (v0) from the linear slope. Calculate % inhibition relative to DMSO vehicle control (0% inhibition) and no-enzyme blank (100% inhibition). Determine IC50 and Ki via 4-parameter logistic sigmoidal fitting.

4. Part B: In Vitro Plasmodium falciparum SYBR Green I Phenotypic Assay

  1. Culture Maintenance: Maintain 3D7 and Dd2 strains in human O+ red blood cells at 2% hematocrit in RPMI-1640 supplemented with 25 mM HEPES, 0.5% Albumax II, and 50 mg/L hypoxanthine under hypoxic gas (5% O2, 5% CO2, 90% N2) at 37 °C.
  2. Synchronization: Synchronize cultures to ring stage with 5% (w/v) D-sorbitol. Dilute synchronized rings to 0.5% initial parasitemia at 2% final hematocrit.
  3. Compound Plating: Dispense 100 μL synchronized parasite culture into black optical 96-well plates containing serial dilutions of test articles (0.05 nM to 10,000 nM). Include untreated infected erythrocytes (100% growth) and 10 μM chloroquine (100% kill).
  4. Incubation: Incubate plates for 72 hours at 37 °C under hypoxic gas.
  5. Lysis & Readout: Add 100 μL lysis buffer (20 mM Tris-HCl, pH 7.5, 5 mM EDTA, 0.008% saponin, 0.08% Triton X-100) containing 2x SYBR Green I dye. Incubate in dark for 60 minutes at 25 °C. Measure fluorescence at Ex 485 nm / Em 535 nm on Tecan Infinite M200 Pro.
