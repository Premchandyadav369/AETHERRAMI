Sample Allocation: All 24 Blinded Test Articles (VAL-CMPD-01 to 24)

1. Purpose & Scope

To establish baseline drug-like properties, Phase I hepatic metabolic clearance, automated patch clamp cardiotoxicity safety margins, and gastrointestinal monolayer permeability.

2. Part A: Hepatic Microsomal Stability (Human & Mouse)

  1. Incubation Setup: 0.5 mg/mL pooled human or mouse liver microsomes in 100 mM potassium phosphate buffer (pH 7.4) with 3.3 mM MgCl2. Add 1.0 μM test compound.
  2. Reaction Initiation: Pre-warm mixture at 37 °C for 5 minutes. Initiate by adding NADPH regenerating system (1.3 mM NADP+, 3.3 mM glucose-6-phosphate, 0.4 U/mL G6PDH).
  3. Time Points: Terminate reactions at t = 0, 15, 30, and 60 minutes by adding 3 volumes of ice-cold acetonitrile containing 100 ng/mL internal standard (tolbutamide).
  4. Centrifugation & Analysis: Centrifuge at 4,000 rpm for 20 minutes at 4 °C. Quantify parent compound depletion via LC-MS/MS. Compute in-vitro half-life (t1/2) and intrinsic clearance (Clint).

3. Part B: Automated Patch Clamp hERG Cardiotoxicity Screening

  1. Cell Preparation: HEK293 cells stably transfected with human KCNH2 (hERG) gene. Detach cells with TrypLE, wash, and resuspend in extracellular recording solution.
  2. Instrument: SyncroPatch 384 or QPatch automated patch-clamp platform.
  3. Voltage Protocol: Holding potential -80 mV; depolarizing pulse to +40 mV for 2,000 ms to activate hERG channels; repolarizing step to -40 mV for 2,000 ms to elicit outward tail current. Tail current amplitude is continuously measured.
  4. Concentration Response: 6-point concentration curve (0.3 μM to 100 μM). Positive control: Dofetilide (100 nM). Cutoff: IC50 > 30 μM (> 300x therapeutic safety margin).

4. Part C: Caco-2 Monolayer Bidirectional Permeability

  1. Culture: Caco-2 cells seeded on 24-well Transwell polycarbonate inserts (0.4 μm pore size) cultured for 21 days until TEER exceeds 400 Ω·cm2.
  2. Transport Assay: Measure Apical-to-Basolateral (A-B) and Basolateral-to-Apical (B-A) transport in HBSS buffer (apical pH 6.5, basolateral pH 7.4) over 120 minutes.
  3. Evaluation: Quantify compound transport by LC-MS/MS. Calculate apparent permeability coefficient (Papp) and Efflux Ratio (ER = Papp B-A / Papp A-B). Target: Papp > 10 x 10-6 cm/s, ER < 2.0.
