1. Executive Overview & Submission Objectives

This dossier contains the complete package for the physical testing, wet-lab synthesis verification, and multi-target biological screening of 24 clinical and preclinical test articles at the Indian Institute of Chemical Technology (CSIR-IICT), Hyderabad. All sample manifests, 96-well microplate layout maps, assay worklists, data entry sheets, and analytical quality release forms have been formatted directly in standard Microsoft Excel workbooks (.xlsx) with pre-coded formulas to ensure seamless, immediate use by CSIR-IICT bench scientists and laboratory technicians.

3. Sample Receipt, Storage & Handling Protocol
  1. Receipt Inspection: Verify intact seals on the 24 sample vials (Barcoded VAL-CMPD-01 to VAL-CMPD-24). Ensure dry ice was maintained throughout shipping.
  2. Long-Term Storage: Store dry powders at -20 °C in a desiccated cabinet protected from moisture and light. Store 10 mM DMSO stocks at -20 °C.
  3. Thawing & Preparation: Thaw DMSO stock solutions at 22-25 °C until completely liquid. Vortex for 15 seconds and sonicate in an ultrasonic bath for 60 seconds before pipetting.
  4. Assay Working Solutions: Prepare serial dilutions in 100% anhydrous DMSO, then dilute into aqueous assay buffer such that the final DMSO concentration is ≤ 0.5% (v/v) to avoid enzyme denaturing or cellular toxicity.
