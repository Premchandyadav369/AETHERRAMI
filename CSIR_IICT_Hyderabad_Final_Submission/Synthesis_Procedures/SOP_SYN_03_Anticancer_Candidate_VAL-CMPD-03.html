Formula & MW: C29H30F2N6O2 | MW: 532.59 g/mol

1. Target Chemistry

VAL-CMPD-03 incorporates a terminal acrylamide warhead positioned for irreversible nucleophilic attack by the thiolate anion of Cysteine-12 in the Switch-II pocket of GDP-bound KRAS G12C.

2. Key Synthetic Steps (1.00 mmol Scale)

  1. Step 1 (Quinazoline Amidation): Condense 2,4,7-trichloroquinazoline with tert-butyl piperazine-1-carboxylate in acetonitrile with DIPEA at 25 °C for 2 hours to selectively substitute the 4-chloro position. (Yield: 88%).
  2. Step 2 (Suzuki-Miyaura Coupling): Couple Intermediate 1 with 2-fluoro-6-hydroxyphenylboronic acid in 1,4-dioxane/water (4:1) using Pd(dppf)Cl2 (5 mol%) and K2CO3 (2.5 eq) at 90 °C for 4 hours. (Yield: 76%).
  3. Step 3 (Boc Deprotection & Acryloylation): Remove Boc protecting group using 4 M HCl in dioxane. Neutralize with DIPEA in dry DCM at -10 °C, and add acryloyl chloride (1.05 eq) dropwise over 10 minutes. Stir at 0 °C for 45 minutes.
  4. Step 4 (Purification): Quench with saturated NaHCO3, extract with DCM, wash with brine, and purify via flash chromatography (DCM/MeOH 95:5). Isolated yield: 388 mg (73%), pale yellow powder. Purity > 98.9%.
