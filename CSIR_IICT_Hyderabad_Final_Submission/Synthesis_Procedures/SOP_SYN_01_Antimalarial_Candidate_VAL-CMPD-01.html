1. Chemical Architecture & Mechanism

Compound VAL-CMPD-01 features a 2,4-diaminotriazine catalytic antifolate headgroup linked via a sterically congested 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)phenyl sulfonamide spacer to a flexible 3-fluoro-4-methoxyphenylpropyl lipophilic tail. This design accommodates the bulky S108N sidechain of pyrimethamine-resistant mutant PfDHFR enzymes while engaging hydrophobic subpocket residues.

3. Step-by-Step Laboratory Procedure

  1. Step 1 (Nucleophilic Aromatic Substitution): Under argon, combine 2,4-diamino-6-chloro-1,3,5-triazine (153 mg, 1.05 mmol) and 4-amino-3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)aniline (229 mg, 1.00 mmol) in dry DMF (4.0 mL). Add DIPEA (0.35 mL, 2.0 mmol). Heat at 105 °C for 6 hours. Monitor by TLC (DCM/MeOH 9:1, Rf = 0.28). Cool to 25 °C, pour into ice water (25 mL), filter, wash with cold water and ether. Dry under vacuum at 45 °C. (Yield: 285 mg, 84%).
  2. Step 2 (Chlorosulfonylation): Cool Intermediate 1 (285 mg) to 0 °C. Add chlorosulfonic acid (0.28 mL, 4.2 mmol) dropwise over 15 minutes. Warm to 60 °C and stir for 3 hours. Quench slowly onto 30 g crushed ice. Filter the sulfonyl chloride intermediate and dissolve in dry THF (5.0 mL).
  3. Step 3 (Sulfonamide Coupling): Add 3-(3-fluoro-4-methoxyphenyl)propan-1-amine (202 mg, 1.10 mmol) and DIPEA (0.44 mL) to the THF solution at 0 °C. Stir at 25 °C for 4 hours. Evaporate THF under reduced pressure.
  4. Step 4 (Purification): Purify crude residue by flash chromatography on silica gel (DCM/Methanol gradient: 98:2 to 90:10). Recrystallize from hot ethanol/water (3:1). Dry under high vacuum at 50 °C for 16 hours. Isolated yield: 452 mg (74% overall), off-white crystalline solid. Purity by HPLC > 99.4%.
