1. Chemical Architecture & Rationale

Compound VAL-CMPD-02 is a bi-aryl ether derivative optimized to bind directly to the NAD+ substrate-binding cleft of InhA. The 1-methylpyrazole heterocycle forms pi-pi stacking interactions with Phe149 and Tyr158 without requiring metabolic activation by mycobacterial KatG.

3. Step-by-Step Laboratory Procedure

  1. Step 1 (SnAr Ether Formation): Charge a 50 mL dry flask with 2,5-dichloronicotinamide (382 mg, 2.00 mmol), 4-(1-methyl-1H-pyrazol-4-yl)phenol (366 mg, 2.10 mmol), and anhydrous K2CO3 (553 mg, 4.00 mmol) in dry DMSO (6.0 mL). Heat at 110 °C for 5 hours under nitrogen. Monitor by TLC (EtOAc/Hexane 1:1, Rf = 0.45).
  2. Step 2 (Isolation): Cool to room temperature. Dilute with cold water (30 mL) and extract with ethyl acetate (3 x 20 mL). Wash combined organic layers with water (2 x 15 mL) and brine (15 mL). Dry over anhydrous Na2SO4, filter, and evaporate to dryness.
  3. Step 3 (Recrystallization): Recrystallize crude solid from ethyl acetate/hexane (1:2). Filter and dry under vacuum at 45 °C for 12 hours. Isolated yield: 535 mg (78%), white crystalline solid. Purity by HPLC > 99.1%.
