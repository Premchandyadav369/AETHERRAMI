Formula & MW: C23H32F3N5O4 | MW: 499.53 g/mol

1. Target Chemistry

VAL-CMPD-04 features a nitrile warhead that engages the catalytic Cys145 of SARS-CoV-2 Mpro in a reversible covalent thioimidate adduct, paired with a bicyclic proline mimetic P2 group.

2. Key Synthetic Steps (1.50 mmol Scale)

  1. Step 1 (Peptide Coupling): Couple (1R,2S,5S)-6,6-dimethyl-3-azabicyclo[3.1.0]hexane-2-carboxylic acid with the trifluoroacetyl-tert-leucine building block using HATU (1.1 eq) and DIPEA (2.5 eq) in DMF at 25 °C for 3 hours. (Yield: 82%).
  2. Step 2 (P1 Gln-lactam Coupling): Couple Intermediate 1 with (S)-2-amino-3-((S)-2-oxopyrrolidin-3-yl)propanamide using EDCI/HOBt in DMF. (Yield: 79%).
  3. Step 3 (Burgess Dehydration to Nitrile): Treat the terminal carboxamide with Burgess reagent (1.5 eq) in dry DCM at 25 °C for 2 hours to dehydrate cleanly to the primary nitrile warhead.
  4. Step 4 (Purification): Purify by automated C18 preparative HPLC (MeCN/Water gradient 20% to 70%). Lyophilize to obtain 576 mg (77%), white fluffy solid. Purity > 99.5%.
